Your music

Import hundreds of tracks at once. Mangawave listens to each one (tempo, energy, brightness, major or minor) and sorts it into a mood; folders named after a mood, like “sad” or “epic”, are respected. Anime-style starter pack: 72 free songs (itch.io). Other free sources: Pixabay Music, Free Music Archive, OpenGameArt, Kenney. Check each track’s license. Moods with no tracks use generated ambient sound.

Albums for manga

Hand-picked itch.io packs. Download one, unzip it, then use “Import a folder” in the Music tab. Always check each page’s license, and use the vocal filter if you want instrumentals only.
